Fold the paper cup

Instruction sheet. You need one square of paper. Five steps.

  1. 1

    Make a triangle

    Lay the square flat. Fold one corner up to the opposite corner, so the square becomes a triangle. Keep the long folded edge nearest to you.

  2. 2

    Fold the left corner across

    Take the left corner and fold it over to touch the middle of the right edge. The top of this flap should lie level.

  3. 3

    Fold the right corner across

    Take the right corner and fold it over to touch the left side, on top of the first flap.

  4. 4

    Fold the top flaps down

    At the top there are two loose points. Fold the front one down over the front, then turn the paper over and fold the other one down over the back.

  5. 5

    Open the cup and stand it up

    Push the top edges apart to open the cup. Stand it on the table and drop a coin or a pen cap in it to show it holds.
